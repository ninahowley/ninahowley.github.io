Observe: Cosmic-Horror Short Story

"I’m such a massive fan of the horror genre, and your story does it justice and then some!"
- Student 1

"Your story was so immersive I could not stop reading it. And, I wasn’t able to predict what was going to happen next, which was really exciting. Each of the elements felt unexpected, yet enhanced the story so much."
- Student 2

"This story offers an intriguing cosmic horror premise that unfolds through a quiet, character-driven encounter with the unknown."
- Professor

This the piece that I submitted for my final critique in ENG 203: Short Narrative.

I wrote this story in 24 hours, after pivoting away from Litter (see above).

I love horror, thriller and mystery media, so I wanted to create a story with these genres. My intention was to build mystery around the situation through the different arcs and interaction with mundane objects. The story relies on the main character’s inner thoughts and dialogue to drive the narrative, hopefully creating uncertainty and suspense. I didn’t necessarily choose a message / moral for my story to have, I just wanted to have fun making it. I think you can attribute a variety of meanings to it. It includes themes of being nervous, or perceiving yourself as being nervous and how this can limit your experiences. The characters are meant to be contrasting figures, where Michelle is brave and curious, the main character would rather stay out of the way and never learn the truth if it means everything can stay the same and no harm is done. The ending / what happens to Michelle isn’t specified to be good or bad, so you can interpret it anyway.
